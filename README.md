# Hi, I'm Ife 👋

I'm a **clinician scientist** based in Lexington, Kentucky. I hold a **PhD** and **post-graduate training in Adult-Gerontology Acute Care** from the University of Kentucky College  and practise as a hospitalist at UK HealthCare. My research covers **cardiovascular outcomes, mHealth engagement, and digital health**. I also build **self-hosted AI and data infrastructure** that turns clinical and research data into models, tools, and automated workflows.

## 🔭 What I'm working on

- **Cardiovascular & heart failure research:** exercise, sedentary behaviour, and CVD risk in rural Appalachia; outcomes in patients with heart failure
- **Digital health & mHealth:** measuring patient engagement with mHealth interventions, wearables, and real-world app use for self-care
- **Applied biostatistics:** survey-weighted regression (HINTS), survival analysis, mediation modelling, and mixed-methods/qualitative analysis
- **Clinical AI & agentic automation:** LLM workflows, RAG over a personal knowledge base, and multi-agent tooling with [Claude Code](https://www.claude.com/product/claude-code) on my own VPS stack
- **ProData Analytics:** founder; research methods and analytics support for graduate researchers and doctoral candidates
